Free match cut video maker

MatchCut makes text match cut and photo match cut videos in your browser. Type a word, or add a photo, and it flies through real-looking newspapers, magazines, books, posters and web pages. Add camera, paper and whoosh sounds, then export a vertical video for TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts.

What is a match cut?

A match cut joins two shots by a shared shape, position or idea. In a text match cut, one word stays in the same spot on screen while the page behind it changes again and again.

Is MatchCut free?

Yes. MatchCut is free, needs no account and adds no watermark by default.

Are my photos or videos uploaded?

No. Everything runs in your browser. Your text, photos and videos stay on your device.

Which video formats can I export?

MP4 in Chrome, Edge and Safari, and WebM in Firefox. Choose 9:16 for TikTok, Reels and Shorts, or 4:5, 1:1 and 16:9. Export at 720p or 1080p.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After your first visit you can install MatchCut as an app and use it without internet.

Can I use it for TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts?

Yes. Export a 9:16 video, then add a trending song in the TikTok or Instagram app if you want one.
